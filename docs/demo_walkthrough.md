# Rankoya demo walkthrough

A guide to showing Rankoya at its best, written for the marketer recording the demo video. It follows the order a real customer would use the product, says what to click, and gives the one line that explains why each step matters.

**The story in one sentence:** Rankoya shows you whether Google and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity) recommend your business, fixes what's stopping them, and writes and publishes the content that wins you more customers. You approve; Rankoya does the work.

---

## Before you record

| Prepare | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A demo account created on the backend (sign-up is switched off for now) | You'll sign in with email and password |
| A real website you're allowed to use, ideally a small business site with 20 to 200 pages | Every number on screen is live data from that site; nothing is mocked |
| A test WordPress.com, Ghost or Webflow blog connected to that site | So you can publish an article live on camera |
| A Google account that owns the site in Google Search Console | So Rankings shows real clicks and searches |
| Run the slow steps the day before (site scan, keyword discovery, AI visibility test, competitor research) | Some take several minutes; on the day you show results, not progress bars |
| Light theme, browser zoom 110%, notifications off | Clean, readable footage |

The page tour starts by itself for accounts created that day. To show it on an older account, turn on **Page tour** in the profile menu (top right).

---

## 1. Sign in (15 seconds)

1. Open the app. Show the sign-in page and the language menu (top right): Rankoya is fully translated into English and Arabic, and Google translates it into 131 more, including French, Spanish and Italian.
2. Sign in with email and password. Mention **Forgot password?**: it emails a reset link.

> Say: "Rankoya works in your language, and your team signs in with the accounts you set up for them."

## 2. Pick or add a site (1 minute)

1. The **Sites** page lists every website in the workspace. **Add a site** is at the top.
2. Click **Add a site** and enter the domain. Walk through setup quickly:
   - **Details:** Rankoya reads the site and drafts its description and audience. You only confirm.
   - **Google Search Console:** connect it (recommended) or skip.
   - **Core topics:** pick up to five topics, or click **Use recommended**. On a brand-new site with no topics yet, use **Skip for now**.
   - **Publishing, preferences, done:** choose where articles go and how many a week. The summary shows what's set up and what was skipped.

> Say: "Setup takes five minutes. Rankoya reads your site and does the research; you just confirm."

To keep the video short, add the site live, then switch to the site you prepared earlier with its data ready.

## 3. Overview: the whole picture (30 seconds)

Open the site. The **Overview** shows how easy you are to find on Google and in AI answers this month, and what to do next.

> Say: "One screen: are people finding you, and what should you do about it today?"

## 4. Integrations: connect once (45 seconds)

1. Open **Integrations** (it's near the top of the sidebar on purpose).
2. Show the connected website (WordPress.com, Ghost, Webflow…) with its status, and **Search Console**.
3. Open Search Console: the **Google search performance** card shows clicks, impressions, click rate, average position and the top searches bringing people in. It syncs automatically after you pick the property, and **Sync now** refreshes it.

> Say: "Connect your website and Google once. From then on Rankoya publishes for you and works from your real data."

## 5. Rankings: where you stand on Google (30 seconds)

1. Open **Rankings**. The Google card at the top shows real Search Console numbers.
2. Below it are the searches you track, your position for each and how it's moving. Switch between desktop and mobile.
3. Use the **Close to page one** filter: these are the quickest wins.

> Say: "Page one is where the clicks are. Rankoya shows which searches you're closest to winning."

## 6. AI visibility: the headline feature (1.5 minutes)

1. Open **AI visibility**. Explain: "When someone asks ChatGPT what to buy, does it recommend you?"
2. Show the list of questions your customers ask. Each one shows which assistants mention you (their logos light up with a green tick) and when it was last tested.
3. Tick two or three questions, pick one assistant (for example ChatGPT), and click **Test**. Or open a single question's **Test** menu and test it on every assistant.
4. Open a question to see each assistant's actual answer, who it recommended and which websites it cited.
5. Point out the gaps: questions where competitors are named and you aren't.

> Say: "This is the new search. If AI assistants don't know you, you're invisible to a growing share of buyers."

## 7. Competitors: why they win (1 minute)

1. Open **Competitors**. The three-step guide at the top explains the page.
2. **The table** puts you next to each competitor in Google and AI answers. Hover a column name to see what it means. **Visit site** opens their website.
3. **Close the gaps:** questions they win in AI answers (click **Write answer page** and Rankoya drafts it) and searches they rank for and you don't (one click adds the keyword).
4. **Get the links they have:** click **Find link prospects** next to a competitor. Rankoya finds the websites that link to them and adds them to your Authority page as sites to contact.

> Say: "See exactly what your competitors do that you don't, and close each gap in one click."

## 8. Site audit: fix what's holding you back (1.5 minutes)

1. Open **Site audit**. The scores show SEO health and AI readiness.
2. **Issues to fix** are grouped by problem, most important first. Each issue says whether **Rankoya** can fix it or **you** need to.
3. For Rankoya's fixes, click **Let Rankoya fix**, then approve. Open one issue to show the proposed fix, with a **Copy** button on the new value and on any code.
4. For your fixes, the issue page shows the recommended value with a Copy button and step-by-step instructions.
5. Open the **Money pages** tab: the pages that make you money (services, products, pricing), each with what would help it rank and sell better.
6. **All pages** shows every page Rankoya read, with a live preview.

> Say: "Rankoya finds what's stopping Google and AI from trusting your site, and fixes most of it for you, with your approval."

## 9. Keywords and topics (45 seconds)

1. Open **Keywords**. Every search the site could win, with volume, difficulty and whether it's worth targeting.
2. Switch to **Topical map**: keywords grouped into topics, and which topics already have a page.
3. Click **Research** on a keyword: Rankoya reads what's ranking today and builds the brief.

> Say: "Rankoya doesn't guess what to write. It finds the searches you can win and groups them into topics Google rewards."

## 10. Articles: written, linked, published (2 minutes)

1. Open **Articles** and an article Rankoya wrote. Show the editor, the score and the checklist.
2. Point out the **internal links**: links to your own pages are underlined in the text. In the **Internal links** card, **View live** opens the linked page and **Show in article** jumps to where the link is.
3. In the publish panel, choose the destination (each shows its logo and site address).
4. Choose **Publish as Live** or **Draft**. A draft lands on your site unpublished, so you can check it there first. For Shopify, pick the blog.
5. Click **Approve and publish**. When it's live, **View live article** opens the published page, and the publishing card shows the live URL with **Copy URL**.
6. If the site has no API connection, show the copy-and-paste kit: one click copies the title, description, HTML or text.
7. Open **Articles → Publishing history** for every publish attempt across the site.

> Say: "From research to a live, internally linked article on your site, and you approve every one."

## 11. Content calendar (20 seconds)

Open **Content calendar**: the month's plan, what's published and what's waiting for you. Reschedule or swap any article.

> Say: "A steady publishing plan without the spreadsheet."

## 12. Authority (30 seconds)

Open **Authority**: how much other websites trust you, measured by who links to you, and the link opportunities worth going after. Each opportunity moves from **New** to **Worth contacting**, **Contacted** and **Link won**.

> Say: "Links from trusted sites are still one of Google's strongest signals. Rankoya shows which ones to go after."

## 13. Ask Rankoya (30 seconds)

Click **Ask Rankoya** (top right). Ask: "What should I fix first this week?" Then ask it to write something. It answers from your site's data. The same assistant works on Telegram.

> Say: "An SEO expert that knows your site, available any time, including on your phone."

## 14. Free SEO tools (20 seconds)

Open **Free SEO tools**. Each tool says why it matters and when to use it. Run the page title check on a competitor's homepage.

> Say: "Free checks for any page, yours or a competitor's. They never change anything."

## 15. For agencies and resellers (20 seconds, optional)

Show **Agency resources** (sales and delivery kits) and **Resellers** (client accounts), plus the workspace switcher for managing many clients. On plans without these, the pages explain how to unlock them.

## Things to avoid on camera

- Starting a site scan, keyword discovery or AI visibility test and waiting for it. Run them beforehand.
- Shopify connections made with short-lived tokens: they stop working after about 24 hours.
- WordPress.com connections made while the backend redirect is being fixed: confirm the flow works the morning of the recording.
- Data from a site that isn't yours or your client's.
